## My Recommendation

**Build 2 (7'0" Balanced)** is the safest competitive choice because:
1. 94 DREB is still elite — you'll grab boards over most matchups at 7'0" with 82 vertical
2. 80 3PT forces the opposing center to guard you on the perimeter, opening driving lanes
3. 91 Strength means you won't get pushed around
4. 87 Pass Accuracy gives you Gold Break Starter for outlet passes

**Build 1 (6'11" Hyper)** is better if you want max boards (99) and don't care about shooting — your team needs to have spacing covered.
