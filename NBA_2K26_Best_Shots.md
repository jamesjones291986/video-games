## Go-To Shots

The go-to shot functions more as a dribble-into-shot animation — it's about creating separation and triggering speed boosts, not just the release itself.

### For Guards & Wings (6'5"–6'9")

| Animation | Requirement | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| **Jaylen Suggs** | — | Best for guards/wings. Use as a dribble animation — snatchback into speed boost combo. Creates quickness and separation. |
| Klay Thompson | 83 3PT | High release point, very hard to contest. Perfect for catch-and-shoot. |
| Devin Booker | 86 3PT | Fast, easy to time. Works off the catch and off the dribble. Available up to 6'9". |
| Kevin Durant | 89 3PT | High release point, butter-smooth. Available 6'5" and up. Best tall-player option. |

### For Big Men (6'10"+)

| Animation | Requirement | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| **Kevin Durant** | 87 3PT | Best for big men. Compensates for limited movement, dribble into speed boost combo tricks defense. |
| Nikola Jokic | 85 3PT | Smooth for true centers. Unique arc, easy to time. 6'10"+. |
| Bam Adebayo | 82 3PT | Budget option for two-way bigs. Solid high release. |
| LaMarcus Aldridge | 83 3PT | Old-school, high release, hard to contest. |
