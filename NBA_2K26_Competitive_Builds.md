> Last Updated: May 14, 2026

## Position 1: Point Guard — PG Lock

### Physical Profile
| Attribute | Recommendation |
|-----------|---------------|
| Height | 6'2" – 6'3" |
| Weight | 170–180 lbs (maximize speed/agility) |
| Wingspan | +2 to +3 above default (defensive reach without killing shooting) |

### Minimum Attribute Targets
| Attribute | Target | Why |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| Perimeter Defense | 95+ | HOF Challenger, HOF On-Ball Menace |
| Ball Handle | 86+ | Silver Ankle Assassin, pro dribble moves |
| Steal | 91+ | Gold Glove |
| Agility | 84+ | HOF On-Ball Menace requirement |
| Speed | 85+ | Gold Slippery Off-Ball |
| 3PT Shot | 70–75 | Enough for open catch-and-shoot |
| Pass Accuracy | 75+ | Silver Break Starter minimum |

### Priority Badges
| Badge | Target Tier | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Challenger | HOF | 95 Perimeter Defense |
| On-Ball Menace | HOF | 96 PD + 84 Agility |
| Glove | Gold | 91 Steal |
| Interceptor | Gold | 85 Steal |
| Pick Dodger | Gold | 90 PD + 79 Agility |
| Unpluckable | Silver | 80 Ball Handle |
| Handles for Days | Silver | 81 Ball Handle |

### Role
Primary perimeter defender and ball-pressure specialist. Forces turnovers, disrupts opposing PGs, and pushes pace with outlet passes. Shooting is secondary — you score on open catch-and-shoot or transition.

## Season 5 Meta Notes

1. **PF is the most impactful position** — the Playmaking Eraser PF creates the most matchup problems
2. **6'7"–6'9" wings dominate** — positionless basketball is king
3. **90+ Agility + 99 3PT potential** is the magic formula for wings
4. **+1 Cap Breaker system** — you can push 2 attributes one point higher; use this to hit badge thresholds you'd otherwise miss (e.g., 92 → 93 3PT for Gold Limitless)
5. **Stretch Centers > Paint Beasts** in most team compositions
6. **Defensive specialists (Locks)** at PG and SF are extremely valuable in comp
7. **Break Starter at HOF (93 Pass Acc)** is elite on any big — outlet passes create easy transition buckets
8. **Rebirth system** unlocks maximum badge potential — plan for it
